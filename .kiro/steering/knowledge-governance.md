# Knowledge governance

Rules for notes and evidence. Details in `docs/knowledge-governance.md`.

- Every substantive note has a stable concept or recipe id, a learning objective, an original explanation, source references, engine and version, evidence references, verification status, known limitations, related concepts, a last verification date and revalidation triggers.
- Unverified claims are not facts. Only `verified` notes are eligible to be indexed as established facts.
- Keep human-authored reflections separate from agent-generated text. Never edit the human reflections section.
- Do not build a vector database, embedding pipeline or RAG service in Phase 0.1.
- Do not copy book prose. Link to upstream paths instead.
