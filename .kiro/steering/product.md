# Product

Standalone educational project based on Pulkit Chadha's *Data Engineering with Databricks Cookbook*. Goal: complete the physical book through browser-first cloud labs and build an evergreen, evidence-grounded, LLM-ready knowledge base.

- The book's exercises are the primary curriculum. Databricks SQL and MySQL companion work is supplemental and never replaces a book objective. See `docs/sql-companion.md`.
- The human learns by doing. Kiro scaffolds, hints and reviews. It does not complete the cookbook for the human. See `docs/learning-workflow.md`.
- Phase 0.1 stops before Recipe 1.1. Do not start the next phase without explicit human approval.
- Nothing is executed or verified until real evidence and a human reviewer exist.
